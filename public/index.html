FAQ

Questions, Answered.

How do I download a TikTok video without a watermark?

Paste the TikTok link into the box above and hit Grab It. Turn on the watermark-removal toggle before downloading and you'll get a clean video file, ready to repost or edit.

Is GrabIt really free?

Yes — every feature is free with no download limits, no account, and no sign-up. There are no premium tiers and no locked qualities.

Can I download YouTube videos in 4K?

Yes. After pasting a YouTube link, pick 2160p (4K) from the quality list if the video was uploaded in 4K. You can also grab 1080p, 720p, 480p, or audio-only MP3.

Do I need to install an app or extension?

No. GrabIt runs entirely in your browser — paste a link on any phone, tablet, or computer and download. Nothing to install, nothing to update.

Which sites and platforms are supported?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, Pinterest, Vimeo, Twitch, Sora 2, SoundCloud, and Imgur — videos, reels, shorts, stories, clips, and audio.

Is it legal to download videos?

Downloading is fine for your own content or videos the creator allows you to save. Don't re-upload other people's work without permission, and always respect copyright law and each platform's terms of service. GrabIt is strictly for personal use.
