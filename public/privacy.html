Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

GrabIt is designed to collect as little data as possible. This page explains in plain language what happens when you use the service.

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details.
  • No tracking pixels or ad trackers. We don't sell data because we don't collect data to sell.
  • Basic server logs. Like almost every website, our hosting provider keeps short-term technical logs (IP address, request time, error messages) to keep the service running and block abuse. These are not linked to your identity and are rotated automatically.

How downloads work

When you paste a video link, our server fetches the video from the source platform and passes it to your browser for download. We do not keep copies of downloaded videos, and we do not build profiles of what you download.

Cookies

GrabIt does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies present are strictly technical (for example, keeping the connection secure).

Third parties

Video metadata and files come from the platform you pasted a link from (e.g. YouTube, TikTok). Those platforms have their own privacy policies. Font files are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your rights

Since we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing to export or delete. If you have a privacy question, contact us at the email listed on the Terms page.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above.
